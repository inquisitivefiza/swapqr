About SwapQR

Scan. Swap. Connect.

Version 1.0.0

What it does

SwapQR turns your contact details — name, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub — into one QR code. Show it to share, scan one to save. No typing, no typos.

Why it exists

Spelling your name out at an interview. Typing a GitHub handle at a hackathon and hoping it's right. Explaining which profile is actually yours because a few people share your name. SwapQR skips all of that.

How it works

  • Scans with any phone's default camera — no app needed on the other side
  • LinkedIn and GitHub links are tappable, ready to open
  • Scan someone else's code and their details save straight into the app

Built for

Developers and professionals connecting at interviews, hackathons, and conferences.
